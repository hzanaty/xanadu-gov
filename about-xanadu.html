Our nation

From independence to a modern public service

Xanadu is a young sovereign nation founded on dignity at work, shared prosperity and public institutions that earn trust through clear service.

17 January 2026

Independence

After generations of regional administration, the Founding Charter established the sovereign Republic of Xanadu. It committed the new nation to representative institutions, equal citizenship, social protection and an economy open to responsible investment.

2 February 2026

Queen Olivia selected

During the Constitutional Assembly, civic councils from every province selected Olivia as Xanadu's first constitutional Queen and guardian of the Founding Charter. Her role is to protect institutional continuity and represent the Republic at home and abroad.

10 February 2026

Founding Cabinet formed

Queen Olivia and the Council of State formed Xanadu's first Cabinet, bringing together the national portfolios for finance, human resources, digital affairs, infrastructure and international cooperation.

1 May 2026

US$1 billion dedicated to national investment

The Cabinet launched the Future Xanadu Investment Program, dedicating US$1 billion to national infrastructure, workforce skills, digital public services and international partnerships capable of accelerating the young country's development.

18 August 2026

Workforce modernization partnership with SAP

Following its national investment commitment, the Republic of Xanadu signed a memorandum of understanding with SAP covering digital skills, public-service innovation and employer readiness. The partnership supports Xanadu's ambition to build modern institutions and a future-ready workforce.
