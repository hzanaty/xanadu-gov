Central District HR Bureau relocates to new premises

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources · Public Service Notice

The Central District bureau will operate from 24 Founders Avenue from 1 October 2026.

The move is part of a routine facilities upgrade. Public counter services will continue without interruption, and existing appointments remain valid.

The new premises provide step-free access, more consultation rooms and additional waiting capacity. Telephone numbers, service hours and online channels are unchanged.

No regulatory effect

This notice concerns only the bureau's physical address. It does not change any employment rule, employer filing obligation or social-insurance procedure.
