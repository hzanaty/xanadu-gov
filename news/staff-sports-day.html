Ministry holds annual staff sports day at headquarters

Ministry staff news

Employees from across departments joined the Ministry's annual sports day at the headquarters grounds.

The program included friendly team competitions, a group walk and voluntary health checks. The event promotes wellbeing and cooperation among Ministry personnel.

The Ministry thanks the organizing committee, volunteers and participating teams. Public services operated normally throughout the event.

Internal event

This staff news item has no effect on employment law, payroll, social insurance or public services.
