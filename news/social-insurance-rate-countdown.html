05 September 2026Employer alert

One week remaining before revised social insurance contribution rates take effect

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources · Reference MoHR/SI/2026/114

The Ministry reminds all registered employers that the social insurance contribution rates enacted by Royal Decree No. 18/2026 take effect on 12 September 2026. Employers should complete payroll preparations without delay.

Implementation reminder

Underlying change
Royal Decree No. 18/2026
Amended law
Social Insurance Act No. 7/2026, Article 14
Effective date
12 September 2026
New shares
Employer 8.5%; employee 5.5%
First affected cycle
Payroll with a payment date on or after the effective date

Action required this week

Payroll and HR systems that still apply the previous employer rate of 7% or employee rate of 5% must be updated before the first affected payroll is processed. Employers should confirm that contribution bases, rate tables, deductions and employer costs calculate correctly.

Employers using an external payroll provider should obtain confirmation that the revised 8.5% employer share and 5.5% employee share will be applied on time. Test results should be retained with payroll control records.

This is a reminder, not a second legal change

This notice does not introduce new contribution rates or extend coverage. It repeats the change previously enacted and announced under Royal Decree No. 18/2026 and highlights the approaching implementation deadline.

The legal basis, covered population and effective date are unchanged from Ministry notice MoHR/SI/2026/087 of 20 August 2026.

Employer support

Implementation questions may be submitted to the Employer Support Desk. Employers remain responsible for accurate withholding and reporting from the effective date.
