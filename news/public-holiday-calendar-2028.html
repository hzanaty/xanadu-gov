Ministry confirms the official 2028 public holiday calendar for employers

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources · Holiday Calendar Circular No. 31/2026 · Republic of Xanadu

The Ministry of Human Resources has officially issued the national public holiday calendar for 2028, confirming the dates of ten statutory public holidays applicable throughout the Republic of Xanadu.

2028 calendar summary

Calendar year
2028
National holidays
10 statutory public holidays
Applicable from
1 January 2028
Legal basis
Employment Standards Act
New entitlements
None
Holiday pay and substitute days
Existing provisions continue unchanged

The confirmed 2028 schedule is published for employers to prepare annual workforce rosters, update HR and time-management holiday calendars, and align payroll planning with the official national holiday dates.

The Ministry confirms that the annual schedule implements the existing public holiday provisions of the Employment Standards Act. This publication does not increase or reduce the number of statutory holidays, change employee holiday entitlements, amend holiday-pay calculations, or introduce new substitute-day arrangements.

Employers are advised to incorporate the official 2028 holiday dates into their workforce and payroll planning calendars. This circular is a final nationwide calendar publication, not a proposal or a change to existing statutory entitlements.
