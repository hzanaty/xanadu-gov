Reminder: submit outstanding annual leave requests before year-end

Ministry staff notice · Human Resources Directorate

Ministry employees are reminded to submit any remaining annual leave requests through the internal staff portal before 31 December 2026.

Line managers should review pending requests in time to maintain service coverage. Requests and carry-over decisions remain subject to the Ministry's existing internal personnel guidance.

No change to leave entitlement

This is an internal administrative reminder for Ministry staff. It does not amend statutory annual leave, introduce a new deadline for private-sector employers or change any employee entitlement.
