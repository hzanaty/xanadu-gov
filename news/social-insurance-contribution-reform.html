Royal Decree No. 18/2026 revises social insurance contribution rates

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources · Reference MoHR/SI/2026/087

Royal Decree No. 18/2026 has amended Article 14 of the Social Insurance Act No. 7/2026. Revised statutory contribution rates will apply to covered employees from 12 September 2026.

Change at a glance

Legal instrument
Royal Decree No. 18/2026
Amended law
Social Insurance Act No. 7/2026, Article 14
Effective date
12 September 2026
Covered parties
All registered private-sector employers and covered employees
Previous total
12% of insured earnings
New total
14% of insured earnings

What changes

The employer contribution will increase from 7% to 8.5% of insured earnings. The employee contribution withheld through payroll will increase from 5% to 5.5%. The combined statutory contribution will therefore increase from 12% to 14%.

Payroll implementation

Employers must apply the revised rates in the first payroll cycle with a payment date on or after 12 September 2026. Payroll and HR systems must retain the previous rates for payroll cycles with payment dates before the effective date and apply the revised rates only from the effective date.

Where a payroll cycle includes retroactive adjustments, the applicable rate is determined by the original payment date of the adjusted earning. Employers must preserve calculation records for inspection.

Required employer action

Registered employers and payroll providers should update rate tables, validate employer and employee deductions, and test payroll results before processing the first affected cycle. Contribution returns submitted for affected payment dates must reflect the revised shares.

Follow-up communication

The Ministry will issue implementation reminders before the effective date. Such reminders will not create a new rate; they will refer to this same change under Royal Decree No. 18/2026.
