Ministry publishes the 2027 public holiday schedule

Issued by the Ministry of Human Resources · Calendar Notice MoHR/CAL/2026/09

The official national public holiday calendar for 2027 is now available for workforce planning and public reference.

2027 calendar summary

National holidays
10 days
New entitlements
None
Substitute days
Existing arrangements continue
Purpose
Annual planning and public information

Employers may use the calendar when planning work schedules and payroll calendars. Where a national holiday falls on a weekend, the customary substitute-day arrangements under the Employment Standards Act continue to apply.

This annual publication does not change the number of statutory holidays, holiday-pay rules or employee entitlements. It confirms the dates on which existing provisions apply during 2027.
