Our mandate

The first authority for Xanadu's workforce

The Ministry turns the Founding Charter's promise of fair work into practical standards, accessible services and dependable social protection.

Employment standards

Set national rules for employment contracts, working time, leave, wages and workplace protections.

Social insurance

Develop contribution policy, issue employer guidance and coordinate administration with the Social Insurance Authority.

Skills & participation

Expand workforce participation, apprenticeships, digital skills and pathways into emerging industries.

How the Ministry serves

To create a labor market where clear rules, secure protection and productive enterprise reinforce one another.

The Ministry prepares employment and social-protection legislation, publishes binding and explanatory notices, maintains employer services and monitors implementation. It works with the Royal Office, the Council of State, the Social Insurance Authority, worker representatives and employer councils.

How we publish

Formal changes are identified by their legal instrument, publication reference and effective date. Follow-up notices may repeat a prior change to help employers prepare, but they do not create a second legal obligation.

Employer readiness

Where a change affects payroll or HR administration, the Ministry states the covered population, required action and implementation date as directly as possible. The Employer Support Desk publishes technical guidance and answers implementation questions.
